# Administration & Content Management Guide

This guide is designed for client Serkan and content managers operating the TRAILER COMMERCE CMS administration panel.

---

## 1. Accessing the Administration Panel

1. Navigate to `/admin/login` or click **CMS ADMIN DEMO** in the website footer or mobile drawer.
2. Enter the demonstration credentials:
   - **Email:** `admin@trailercommerce.demo`
   - **Password:** `TrailerDemo123!`
   *(Or click the "Auto-Fill Demo Credentials" button).*
3. Click **Sign In to Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Managing Products & Trailers

### Editing an Existing Product
1. In the sidebar, click **Products** (`/admin/products`).
2. Locate the trailer model you wish to update and click **Edit**.
3. You can edit:
   - **Title & Slug:** e.g., "Enclosed Cargo 6×12".
   - **Pricing:** Base price and optional sale discount price.
   - **Main Photograph:** Paste a new image URL or pick one of the sample preset thumbnails.
   - **Specifications:** Dimensions, axle configurations, payload capacities, materials, and colours.
   - **Availability:** Toggle between *In Stock*, *Built to Order*, and *Available Soon*.
   - **SEO Metadata:** Adjust the meta title and description while monitoring the real-time Google search snippet preview.
4. Click **Save Changes**. The updated trailer will immediately reflect across the storefront!

### Adding a New Trailer Model
1. Click **+ Add New Trailer** at `/admin/products/new`.
2. Enter the model details. As you type the title, the URL slug and Google SEO snippet preview generate automatically.
3. Click **Publish Product** to make it instantly visible in the public catalogue.

### Duplicating and Deleting Products
- In the Products table, click **Duplicate** to make an instant cloned draft of any trailer.
- Click **Delete** to remove a product with confirmation.

---

## 3. Media Asset Library

Navigate to `/admin/media` to:
- Drag and drop local image files to generate local data URL previews.
- Inspect pixel dimensions, file size, and upload dates.
- Edit SEO alt-text tags for each image.

---

## 4. Editing Static Pages & Copy

Navigate to `/admin/pages` to modify:
- **Homepage:** Main headline, eyebrow, subtitle description, and CTA buttons.
- **About Page:** Company philosophy text and intro paragraph.
- **Contact Page:** Operational phone numbers, enquiry email, and distribution center hours.

---

## 5. Demonstration Reset

If you have made numerous test edits and want to return to the original clean demo state:
1. Go to **Site Settings** (`/admin/settings`).
2. Scroll to the **Demonstration State Control** card.
3. Click **Reset All Data to Factory Seed Defaults**.
